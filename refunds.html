Refunds and cancellation

Effective 3 October 2026

In short: Slowlight Premium is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to cancel. If you are not happy with it, you can ask for a refund through Google Play, or write to us and we will help.

What you can buy

Slowlight is free. The only purchase is Slowlight Premium, an optional one-time unlock sold through Google Play. There is no subscription and no automatic renewal, so there is nothing to cancel.

Asking for a refund

  • Within 48 hours of buying: you can request a refund yourself from Google Play: open play.google.com → Order history, find Slowlight Premium, and choose Request a refund. Google decides these requests.
  • After 48 hours: write to digitallysawan@gmail.com with the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA.) and a line about what went wrong. We aim to reply within 3 working days.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made by Google Play to the payment method you used. How long the money takes to reach you depends on Google Play and your bank or payment app.

After a refund

Premium is removed the next time Slowlight checks with Google Play. Your puzzles keep working, and any Premium sound or look you chose switches back to a free one. Nothing else is lost.
