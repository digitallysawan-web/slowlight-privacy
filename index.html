Slowlight privacy policy

Effective 3 October 2026

In short: Slowlight does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app remembers stays on your phone.

Who we are

Slowlight is an Android app made by Sawan Biswas, an independent developer in India ("we"). For any privacy question, write to digitallysawan@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

To work, Slowlight saves a few things in its own private storage on your device:

  • Your choices: the puzzle, sleep timer and sound you picked last, and your Settings (volume, dimming, motion and each puzzle's look).
  • The bedtime reminder time, only if you turn the reminder on.
  • Whether the short introduction has been shown, so it opens only once.
  • Whether Google Play has confirmed your Premium purchase, so Premium works without a network connection.
  • The puzzle in progress, so you can continue where you left off.

We never receive any of this. Other apps cannot read it.

Android backup: if backup is turned on for your phone, Android may copy your choices and Settings to your own Google account, so they return on a new phone. The puzzle in progress is never backed up. This backup is run by Google under Google's privacy policy; we cannot see it.

What we do not collect

Slowlight does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, files, device identifiers or advertising ID. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software. Slowlight's own code never connects to the internet.

Google Play

  • Purchases: Slowlight Premium is an optional one-time purchase sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment. Slowlight asks the Google Play app on your phone whether your account owns Premium; it never sees your card, UPI or bank details. Google shares with us the standard order details it gives every developer (such as the order number, product, date and country), which we use only for refunds and support.
  • Google Play Billing Library: the purchase feature uses Google's Play Billing Library, which is built into the app. It has internet access and may send Google technical diagnostic information about purchase requests (for example, whether a request succeeded). We do not receive this information.
  • Crash reports: if you have agreed to share usage and diagnostics with Google on your phone, Android may send Google anonymous crash and performance reports, which Google shows us in summary. We cannot link them to you.

Google's handling of this data is described in the Google privacy policy.

Permissions

Slowlight asks you for one permission, and only if you turn on the optional bedtime reminder: notifications (Android 13 and later). Android also lists permissions that are granted without asking:

  • Run in the background and keep the device awake, so the sound you chose keeps playing after the screen turns off, with a notification to pause or stop it.
  • Google Play billing and internet access, used only by Google's Play Billing Library for the optional Premium purchase.
  • Run at startup, only to set the bedtime reminder again after the phone restarts.

None of these is used to collect personal data.

Usage statistics

Slowlight sends no usage statistics of any kind. What we learn about the app comes only from the summary reports Google Play gives every developer (for example, how many people installed it, and in which countries) and from reviews you choose to write.

Who Slowlight is for

Slowlight is meant for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Everything Slowlight stores is on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes all of it. We hold no copy, because we run no servers that receive your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Changes will never add data collection without it being described here first.
