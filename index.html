Slowlight privacy policy

Effective 29 September 2026

In short: Slowlight does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app remembers stays on your phone.

Who we are

Slowlight is an Android app made by Sawan Biswas, an independent developer in India ("we"). For any privacy question, write to digitallysawan@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

To work, Slowlight saves a few things in its own private storage on your device:

  • Your choices: the puzzle, sleep timer, break length and Settings you picked last.
  • The puzzle in progress, so you can continue where you left off.
  • Two small counters: how many calm breaks you have finished, and whether the app has already asked you once for a Google Play rating.

We never receive any of this. Other apps cannot read it.

Android backup: if backup is turned on for your phone, Android may copy your choices, Settings and the two counters to your own Google account, so they return on a new phone. The puzzle in progress is never backed up. This backup is run by Google under Google's privacy policy; we cannot see it.

What we do not collect

Slowlight does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, files, device identifiers or advertising ID. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software, and it does not use the internet for play.

Google Play

  • Ratings: after your third finished calm break, Slowlight may ask Google Play to show its standard rating window, once. Google runs that window; we only see the public rating and review if you choose to leave one.
  • Crash reports: if you have agreed to share usage and diagnostics with Google on your phone, Android may send Google anonymous crash and performance reports, which Google shows us in summary. We cannot link them to you.
  • Purchases (when offered): an optional Premium upgrade will be sold through Google Play. Google handles payment; we receive only confirmation that a purchase is valid, never your payment details.

Google's handling of this data is described in the Google privacy policy.

Permissions

This version asks for no permissions. Later versions may ask for:

  • Notifications, only if you turn on the optional bedtime reminder.
  • Internet, only so Google Play purchases can be checked.

Neither is used to collect personal data.

Usage statistics

Slowlight sends no usage statistics of any kind. What we learn about the app comes only from the summary reports Google Play gives every developer (for example, how many people installed it, and in which countries) and from reviews you choose to write.

Who Slowlight is for

Slowlight is meant for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Everything Slowlight stores is on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes all of it. We hold no copy, because we run no servers that receive your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Changes will never add data collection without it being described here first.
