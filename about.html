Slowlight

Relaxing puzzles for winding down at bedtime · Android

Slowlight gives your mind one small, gentle thing to do when you can't switch off at night. Three calm puzzles, a soft sound if you like, and a dim screen that fades by itself when you're ready to put the phone down.

What's inside

  • Three gentle puzzles: stack stones that always balance, guide a slow snake that never crashes, and fit soft shapes together. No score, no timer, no way to fail.
  • Made for the night: dim, warm colours only, and the screen fades on its own after two quiet minutes.
  • Sounds and a sleep timer: rain, brown noise or night crickets, which fade out by themselves.
  • No ads, no account, and nothing personal leaves your phone.

Price

Slowlight is free to download, and all three puzzles are free with no limits. Slowlight Premium is an optional one-time purchase (₹599 in India; Google Play shows the price in your currency). It adds seven more sounds and four more looks for each puzzle. There is no subscription. Payment is handled by Google Play.
